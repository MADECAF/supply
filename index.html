Terms & Conditions (B2B Green Coffee Sourcing)

1. Scope & Nature of Service

MADECAF is a bilateral green coffee sourcing initiative connecting Malagasy coffee producers with Malaysian coffee roasters. All platform interactions, sample requests, and wholesale inquiries are conducted on a business-to-business (B2B) basis.

2. Quotations & Order Inquiries

Information and sample pricing displayed on this platform are indicative in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). Formal commercial offers and container allocations are subject to written confirmation, crop harvest availability, and bilateral contract agreement.

3. Sample & Wholesale Quantities

Green coffee samples are provided in 1 kg evaluation pouches and 5 kg pilot batches. Wholesale commercial orders require a minimum order quantity (MOQ) of 60 kg (standard jute bag). Custom volume contracts may be negotiated on FOB Toamasina or CIF Port Klang terms.

4. Payment & Trade Terms

Payment terms for sample orders and commercial allocations are agreed upon quotation acceptance, utilizing standard international trade instruments (Telegraphic Transfer, Letter of Credit, or local Malaysian business banking).

* Note: These Terms & Conditions are drafted for academic project review and do not constitute certified legal contracts. Commercial terms remain subject to negotiation.

Delivery Policy & Shipping Guidelines

1. Sample & Pilot Order Delivery (1 kg & 5 kg)

Sample pouches are prepared in sealed barrier packaging to maintain green bean moisture content during transit. Dispatches to West Malaysia are handled via international express air courier (DHL / FedEx / air freight partners), with estimated transit times of 3 to 7 business days from dispatch.

2. Wholesale Shipments (MOQ 60 kg & Container Allocations)

Wholesale orders are packed in standard GrainPro-lined jute bags (60 kg) and transported via scheduled ocean freight from Toamasina, Madagascar to Port Klang (Klang Valley), Malaysia. Maritime transit typically requires 20 to 35 days depending on shipping line schedules.

3. Phytosanitary & Customs Compliance

All green coffee shipments departing Madagascar are accompanied by official Phytosanitary Certificates issued by the Malagasy Ministry of Agriculture, along with Certificate of Origin documentation compliant with Malaysian Department of Agriculture (DOA) import regulations.

4. Estimated Timeframes & Tracking

Delivery timeframes provided are realistic estimates based on normal logistics conditions. Tracking references are provided upon consignment dispatch for real-time monitoring.

* Note: Shipping terms and transit schedules are illustrative academic projections and subject to carrier operations.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Order Cancellation Prior to Dispatch

Sample requests and pilot orders may be cancelled or modified without penalty prior to handover to the international courier. Cancellation notices should be communicated via our official sourcing email or WhatsApp hotline.

2. Sample & Pilot Order Policy

Due to the agricultural and perishable nature of unroasted green coffee beans and international phytosanitary quarantine restrictions, opened or received green coffee samples cannot be returned once delivered in undamaged condition.

3. Damaged or Compromised Consignments

If a sample consignment arrives with compromised packaging, moisture ingress, or physical transit damage, the recipient roastery is requested to submit photographic documentation within 48 hours of receipt. Upon verification, MADECAF coordinates a replacement dispatch.

4. Wholesale Commercial Orders

Wholesale allocations are governed by tailored commercial purchase agreements, including pre-shipment sample approval (PSS) protocols and standard quality arbitration terms prior to port loading.

* Note: This refund and cancellation guideline is developed for academic project simulation and does not represent an executed legal warranty.
